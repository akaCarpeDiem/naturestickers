Every sticker is printed to order just for you, so all sales are final. We don’t accept returns or exchanges for change of mind.

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, email support@naturestickers.shop within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We’ll send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. There’s no need to send anything back, and it won’t cost you anything.

If a package is lost in transit, email us and we’ll help sort it out.

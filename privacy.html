Privacy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Nature Stickers respects your privacy. This page explains what information is handled when you visit naturestickers.shop.

What we collect

  • This site has no accounts, forms or newsletter sign-ups, and it does not ask you for personal information.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them only to reply.
  • Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed) to deliver and secure the site.

Purchases and social platforms

This site does not currently process payments. If you visit or buy from us on Etsy, or follow us on Pinterest or Instagram, those platforms’ own privacy policies apply to the information you share there.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies for security and performance.

Changes

If we start selling directly on this site, we will update this policy before doing so.

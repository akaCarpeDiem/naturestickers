Privacy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Nature Stickers respects your privacy. This page explains what information is handled when you visit or buy from naturestickers.shop.

What we collect

  • This site has no accounts or newsletter sign-ups.
  • Orders. When you check out, payment is handled by Stripe on its secure checkout page. Stripe collects your name, email, card details and shipping address. We never see or store your full card number. We receive your name, email, shipping address and what you ordered so we can fulfil and support the order.
  • Printing and shipping. Orders are printed and shipped by our print partner, Printful. We share your name, shipping address, email (and phone, if you give one) and the items ordered with Printful only so it can make and deliver your stickers and send tracking updates.
  • Your cart. Items you add to the cart are stored in your own browser (local storage) until you check out or clear site data. They are not sent to us until you start checkout.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them only to reply and to help with your order.
  • Hosting and analytics. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed) to deliver and secure the site. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

How we use and share it

We use order information only to process payment, print and ship your order, handle returns or problems, and keep records we are legally required to keep (for example tax and accounting). We share it only with the service providers named above (Stripe, Printful, Cloudflare). We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Etsy and social platforms

If you buy from us on Etsy, or follow us on Pinterest or Instagram, those platforms’ own privacy policies apply to the information you share there.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare and Stripe may set strictly necessary cookies for security, fraud prevention and checkout.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed to fulfil the order, handle returns and meet tax and accounting rules. Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it (except records we must keep by law), by emailing us. You can clear your cart at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser. This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@naturestickers.shop

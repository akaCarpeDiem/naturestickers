Support

How can we help?

We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

If you have any questions or concerns about an order, shipping, returns or anything else, email us at support@naturestickers.shop and we’ll get back to you within 1–2 business days.

support@naturestickers.shopOrders, shipping, returns or anything else →

When you email us

  • Include your order number if you have one, plus the name and email you used at checkout.
  • For damaged or misprinted stickers, a quick photo helps us see what happened.

What we can help with

  • Order status & tracking: orders placed on naturestickers.shop ship via Printful. You’ll get tracking by email when your stickers leave the printer.
  • Damaged or misprinted stickers
  • Address changes
  • Returns and anything else about your order
  • Etsy orders: message us through Etsy, or email us with your order number.
  • Custom sizes & wholesale: see the contact page.
